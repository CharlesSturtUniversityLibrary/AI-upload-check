Quick reference: common AI tool terms

Whether you can upload content also depends on what licence you are agreeing to give the AI platform. Check the tool's current terms - they change regularly.

Microsoft Copilot - Enterprise (CSU staff/student login)
Confirms rights onlyDoes not require you to sublicence uploads and is not used to train the model. Files are stored for 30 days then deleted.
Microsoft Copilot - Free/consumer version
Sublicence requiredYou grant Microsoft a licence to your content, including the right to pass it on to suppliers.
ChatGPT (OpenAI)
Confirms rights onlyYou keep ownership of what you upload, but confirm you have the rights to it. Your content may be used to improve the service unless you opt out in settings.
Claude (Anthropic)
Confirms rights onlyYou keep your rights to inputs. Content may be used for training unless you opt out in account settings.
Google Gemini
Sublicence requiredYou licence Google to use uploaded content to provide and improve its services. Training is on by default but can be switched off in Gemini Apps Activity settings.
Google NotebookLM
Sublicence requiredCovered by Google's general Terms of Service - you licence Google to use content to provide and develop the service.
Perplexity AI
Sublicence requiredYou grant Perplexity a licence to use your content, including to publish output based on it.

For the full picture, including outputs and AI-generated content, see the CSU Library Generative AI guide: Copyright (opens in new tab).

This tool is a quick-reference guide based on CSU Library's Generative AI guide. It does not cover every situation and is not legal advice. Terms of use and licence agreements change, so always check the current source.
